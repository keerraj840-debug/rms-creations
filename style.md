# Style & Design Guide — RMS Creations

> **What is this document?**  
> This guide explains how the **RMS Creations** website looks and feels. It describes the colors, fonts, buttons, cards, and rules used to make the website look like a warm, high-end gift boutique.

---

## 1. How the Website Should Look and Feel

RMS Creations is a boutique gift shop. It should **not** look like a generic corporate website with cold blue colors or plain black text. 

Instead, it should feel:
* **Warm and Festive:** Soft terracotta, warm brown, peach rose, and creamy white tones that remind people of celebrations, sweets, and gift boxes.
* **Neat and High Quality:** High-resolution photos, clean borders, and plenty of breathing space around text so it looks premium.
* **Friendly and Clear:** Big, easy-to-read text, clear price tags in Rupees (`₹`), and friendly rounded buttons that invite you to tap.
* **Great on Mobile Phones:** Over 80% of customers open the website on their phones, so every button, picture, and text size is designed to look great on small screens.

---

## 2. Color Palette (Simple Explanation)

We use a carefully chosen set of warm, earthy colors. Here is what each color does:

### Main Colors
* **Warm Terracotta (`#C56D5B`):**  
  Our main brand color. Used for important buttons (like "Order on WhatsApp"), highlighted text, and active tabs.
* **Dark Chocolate Brown (`#57251C`):**  
  Our primary text color in light mode. Much softer and more luxurious than harsh pure black. Used for headlines and main body text.
* **Peach Rose (`#DD8776`) & Soft Blush (`#EB9D8E`):**  
  Soft pastel shades used for badges, subtle card glows, and promotional banners.
* **Creamy Warm Sand (`#F7F6F3`):**  
  The soft background color of the entire website. Feels much warmer and gentler than sterile hospital white.
* **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):**  
  Used inside product cards and pop-up windows so photos and text stand out clearly.
* **Emerald Green (`#166534`):**  
  Used for the WhatsApp icon and live store status dot so customers immediately recognize it.

### Dark Mode Colors (Night Theme)
When a user switches to dark mode, the website does **not** turn into cold gray. It uses rich, dark chocolate tones:
* **Dark Background:** Deep espresso (`#14100E`).
* **Card Surface:** Warm dark brown (`#251F1C`).
* **Main Text in Dark Mode:** Soft warm cream (`#F4F1ED`) so it never strains the eyes.

## 3. Fonts & Typography (How Text Looks)

We use two main types of fonts to give the site a boutique feel:

1. **Elegant Serif Font (`Playfair Display`):**
   * **Where it is used:** Big headings, hero titles, and product names (e.g., *"Handcrafted Gifts for Every Special Moment"*).
   * **Why:** Looks classic, artistic, and feels like a luxury invitation card.

2. **Clean Modern Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):**
   * **Where it is used:** Regular reading text, buttons, prices, phone numbers, and menus.
   * **Why:** Extremely clean and easy to read, especially on small phone screens.

### Text Size Rules:
* **Big Titles (Hero section):** Large and bold (32px on phone, up to 56px on laptop).
* **Section Titles:** Clear and noticeable (24px to 32px).
* **Product Names:** Bold and prominent (18px to 20px).
* **Descriptions:** Easy reading size (14px to 16px).
* **Badges & Tags:** Small, bold, and uppercase (11px to 12px) with extra letter spacing.

## 4. How Key Parts of the Website Look

### 4.1 Buttons
* **Shape:** Smooth pill shape (fully rounded corners).
* **Main Action Button ("Order on WhatsApp"):**  
  Deep warm terracotta or rich chocolate brown with white text. Slightly enlarges when you hover your mouse, with a little green WhatsApp icon.
* **Secondary Button ("Explore Hampers"):**  
  Clean white or frosted pill with a soft brown border.
* **Mobile Size:** Buttons are tall enough (at least 44 pixels high) so you never miss-tap with your thumb on a mobile screen.

### 4.2 Product Cards
* **Shape:** Rounded corners (like a smooth card).
* **Surface:** Crisp white box with a very faint, soft warm border.
* **Image:** High-quality photo at the top.
* **Hover Effect:** When hovered or tapped, the card gently lifts up with a warm soft shadow.
* **Contents on the card:**
  1. Photo of the gift box
  2. Category tag (e.g., "Ganpati Special", "Kids")
  3. Product title
  4. Small list of what's inside
  5. Price in Rupees (e.g., ₹249)
  6. Green "Order on WhatsApp" button

### 4.3 Top Navigation Menu (Header)
* **Behavior:** Stays gently stuck to the top of the screen as you scroll, so you can always switch pages or track your order.
* **Look:** Frosted glass effect (lets the background softly blur underneath).
* **Includes:**
  * Logo with a small green "Online" dot
  * Links: Home, Ganpati Hampers, Hampers, Return Gifts, Gallery, Top Offers, Contact
  * Dark / Light mode toggle button (Sun / Moon icon)
  * "Track Order" button

### 4.4 Announcement Bar (Top Strip)
* A small strip at the very top of the website with a festive color gradient.
* Shows active announcements (e.g., *"Ganesh Utsav Special: Handcrafted Ganpati Pooja Kits starting at ₹99! Click here to order →"*).

### 4.5 Pop-up Windows (Modals)
* Used for tracking orders or viewing photo details.
* The rest of the screen goes softly dim.
* The pop-up appears in the center with rounded corners and a clear `×` close button at the top right.

---

## 5. Rules for Anyone Making Changes to the Website

1. **Keep it Warm:** Never use pure black (`#000000`), plain harsh red, or electric blue. Stick to our terracotta, blush, and chocolate palette.
2. **Keep it Clean:** Never crowd products together. Leave comfortable margins between cards.
3. **Keep it Mobile-Friendly:** Always check how buttons and pictures look on a regular mobile phone before publishing.
4. **Use Real Photos:** Never use blurry images or generic stock icons where real handcrafted hampers belong.
